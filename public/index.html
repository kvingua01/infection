How to play

Click the world to capture your mouse and aim. Hiders collect glowing cubes to use as power-ups. Late arrivals wait for the next round. For controller play, connect a standard gamepad and press a button. Use the D-pad or left stick to navigate.

Hunters (seekers) have three minutes to catch every hider. They can throw once per second. A hit turns the hider into another hunter, and walls block shots.

Hunters can carry one power-up from a rare red cube. Press F, Xbox X, or PlayStation Square to use it by default. Check Settings for your current bindings.

  • Hunter’s Vision reveals every hider through walls for two seconds, including invisible hiders.
  • Inventory Disruptor destroys one random unused power-up from each hider who has one.
  • Power Nullifier cuts 15 seconds from each active timed hider buff, ending buffs with 15 seconds or less left.

Advanced Instructions

Join a game

Enter a nickname and create or browse a public lobby, or create a private lobby and share its six-character code with friends. The host can start when at least two players are in the lobby. Joining during a round puts you in the waiting room until the next round.

How a round works

One player starts as the seeker. Hiders have 30 seconds to find cover while the seeker is locked away. The hunt then lasts 3 minutes. A projectile hit turns a hider into a seeker who can throw immediately. Seekers win if they catch everyone. Hiders win if any hider survives until time runs out.

Use walls to break sightlines. The underground passages in Giant House and Forest & Cabins have stairs on opposite sides of the map. Learn both exits before using them as hiding spots. Seekers can throw once per second, and walls stop their projectiles.

Controls

Keyboard and mouse. Use WASD to move, Space to jump, and the mouse to aim. Click the world to capture the pointer. Left click throws as a seeker. Use 1 or 2 to select a hider inventory slot and F to use the selected power-up. V switches the camera and Esc opens Settings.

Controller. Use the left stick to move and right stick to aim. Press A or Cross to jump, RT or R2 to throw, and X or Square to use a power-up. D-pad left and right select inventory slots. Menu or Options opens Settings. In menus, use the D-pad to move, A or Cross to select, and B or Circle to go back. Use the right stick to scroll this guide.

These are the defaults. Open Settings to view or change your bindings.

Hider power-ups

You start each round with one random power-up. Walk over glowing cubes to pick them up. You can hold two at a time. Select a slot, then use its power-up when you need it.

Invisibility · 12 seconds
Ordinary seeker vision cannot see you, but projectiles can still hit you and Hunter’s Vision can reveal you. Use it before crossing open ground.
Speed · 15 seconds
Doubles your hider movement speed. Use it to reach cover or put distance between you and a seeker.
Teleport · Instant
Moves you to a random safe spawn location. Useful when a seeker has cut off your escape route.
Decoy · 15 seconds
Creates a moving copy of your character at your position. Send it in one direction while you escape in another.
Freeze · 5 seconds
Stops nearby seekers within 12 blocks from moving or throwing. Use it when a seeker gets too close.
Super Jump · 20 seconds
Increases your jump height so you can reach ledges and cross obstacles more easily.
Smoke · 10 seconds
Creates a smoke cloud at your position that limits nearby seekers’ vision. Use the cover to change direction or move behind a wall.

Hunter power-ups

Rare red cubes are for seekers. There can be one red cube on the map at a time, and you can carry one in your hunter slot. Use the same power-up button to activate it.

Hunter’s Vision · 2 seconds
Marks every remaining hider through walls for all seekers in your lobby, even if a hider is invisible. Look for escape routes as well as positions.
Inventory Disruptor · Instant
Destroys one random unused power-up from each hider who is carrying one. Effects already in use keep running.
Power Nullifier · Instant
Subtracts 15 seconds from each active timed hider buff. A buff with 15 seconds or less remaining ends immediately. Carried items stay in inventory.
